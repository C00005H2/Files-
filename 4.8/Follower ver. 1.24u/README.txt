This script will control multiple clients with some hotkeys from the party leader
Make sure the Aion Client is in english

1. Party leader which you play by yourself will load and play the "Leader - Script ver. 1.xx.pscp"
2. All Party members will play the "Follower - Script ver. 1.xx.pscp"


The Hotkeys are:

Page Down = Wait (All Party Members just wait)

Page Up = Follow (All Party Members follow the Leader including free flight)

End = Combat (All Party Member either attack Leaders target or Heal the entire party)

Home = Loot (All Party Members loot Leaders target)

Delete = Regenerate (All Party Members sit down to regenerate)

Numpad 0 = Quest (With the first key press all Party Members will talk to the leaders target, with
                  the following key presses they will click at the coordinate the leader is hovering above)
